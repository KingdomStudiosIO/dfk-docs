## Apps & Tools

### [Adventures in DeFi Kingdoms (ADFK)](https://www.adfk.app)

Adventures in DeFi Kingdoms is a website, podcast, and Discord community focused on the analysis and strategy of DeFi Kingdoms. We focus on delivering value to our users through data democratization.

Developer: Adventures in DeFi Kingdoms&#x20;

Website: <https://www.adfk.app>

Social Media: [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCEgskSVgL7WBV_cmV-kBt9g)

### [DFK Alerts](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/dfk-alerts/nghiadigonalglomdnbgellmhiecaclh)

DFK Alerts is a Chromium-based extension designed to help DFK players manage their Heroes via browser notifications. It also provides real-time DEX / Tavern floor & in-game token price information.

Developer: Duvel Dev

Website: <https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/dfk-alerts/nghiadigonalglomdnbgellmhiecaclh>

Social Media: [Twitter](https://twitter.com/DFK_Alerts)

### [DFKAnalytics](https://dfkanalytics.com/)

DFKAnalytics is a data visualization tool for DFK. Tools to view the Hero and Pet markets. See what you sold and more! Developed by a DFK community member with love.

Developer: WegHuZ

Website: <https://dfkanalytics.com/>

Social Media: [Twitter](https://twitter.com/Weghuz) | [Discord](https://discord.gg/kuy5tSrH9C)

### [DFK Eggspert](https://www.dfkeggspert.com)

DFK Eggspert is a comprehensive resource for all things DeFi Kingdoms. Sporting Guides, Infographics and Wiki-level detail, we'll get you from Beginner to Eggspert in no time.

Developer: Team Eggspert

Website: <https://www.dfkeggspert.com>

Social Media: [Twitter](https://twitter.com/DFKEggspert) | [Discord](https://discord.gg/cktHnaDTma)

### [Lila's Ledger](https://dfkreport.cognifact.com/)

Exportable DFK Transaction history report with cost basis analysis to assist with tax reporting.

Developer: MellowGreenGiant

Website: <https://dfkreport.cognifact.com/>

### [Rome Terminal](https://app.rometerminal.io)

Rome Terminal is your DIY DeFi Dashboard. Multi-dApp, Multi-Network and always free, you can trade, bridge, research, and analyze your preferred token pairs.

Developer: Rome Blockchain Labs

Website: <https://romeblockchain.com/>

Social Media: [Twitter](https://twitter.com/romeblockchain) | [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/rome-blockchain-labs-inc/)

## Games

### [DFK Arena](https://dfkarena.com/)

Monetize your heroes by pitting them against other heroes. Win large amounts of gold and raffle tickets on victory. Fully animated fights for your unique heroes.

Developer: DFK Arena

Website: <https://dfkarena.com/>

Social Media: [Twitter](https://twitter.com/dfk_arena) | [Discord](https://discord.gg/PgSeuswq82)

### [DFK Fight Klub](https://discord.gg/Ch2mdbgy6Z)

A turn-based combat game that utilizes player skill and leverages your heroes from DeFi Kingdoms. Join our Discord server to learn about monthly tournaments and daily battles with our amazing community.

Developer: TylusDurdenius with ADFK

Discord: <https://discord.gg/Ch2mdbgy6Z>
